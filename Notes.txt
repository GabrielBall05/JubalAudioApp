short description:
Organize, edit, and play your local audio files via seamless playback queue.


full description:
Designed for users who prefer owning their media rather than renting it through subscription services, this app brings a simple user interface and feature-rich queue management of modern music platforms directly to your personal collection of audio files.

Features:
 - Instantly bring in any audio file stored on your device, whether they be songs, voice recordings, podcasts, or audiobooks.
 - Build, reorder, and manage listening sessions with a dynamic playback queue inspired by modern streaming apps. Easily shuffle, add to queue, repeat, reorder, or jump to any track in the timeline.
 - Curate custom playlists to meet your listening purposes.
 - Edit in-app display details such as title, creator, and image to control how your media looks.
 - Powered by Google's industry standard ExoPlayer, enjoy smooth and battery-efficient playback performance across a wide variety of audio formats in either the background or foreground.
 - Blends into your device's aesthetic by dynamically adapting to the system's color scheme using Material You.
end full description
